# Gate records: data-quality report (11 Oct 2026)

Read-only review of `container_visits`. **Nothing in production was changed.**
Every number below comes from the queries in [`checks.sql`](checks.sql), which
anyone with SQL-editor access can re-run. Driver names are deliberately left
out of this file; run queries 4 and 5 to see them.

Scope: 353 visits (65 gated out), 400 driver-name entries (gate-in plus
gate-out).

## Findings

### 1. Visits with no driver: 9 (query 1)

Tickets **#1–#9**, gated out on 16 Jul 2026 (8) and 17 Jul 2026 (1) against
booking **"To ACT"**. Both the gate-in and the gate-out driver are empty; the
truck numbers are recorded.

These look like history entered after the fact, not gate traffic: all nine
were created by the super admin, every gate-in and gate-out time is exactly
12:00, there is no activity-log entry, no gate-out operator and no seal.

### 3. Truck numbers that aren't a 7-digit local plate: 11 (query 3)

343 of 353 gate-in plates and 64 of 65 gate-out plates are 7 digits. The rest:

| Shape | Count | Tickets |
|---|---|---|
| 6 digits (one is `000000`) | 3 | #38, #116, #117 |
| 5 digits | 2 | #268, #344 |
| letters and digits | 3 | #282, #299, #312 |
| letters only | 3 | #58 (both sides, `UNKNOWN`), #332 (a word, not a plate) |

Letters mixed with digits can be foreign plates and may well be right.

### 4. Driver names that differ only by case or spacing: 22 groups (query 4)

The 400 entries hold 199 spellings as typed, but only 173 once case and
spacing are ignored. 302 entries are in capitals, 83 in lower case, and 13
have stray spaces.

### 5. Possible spelling variants: 28 groups (query 5)

28 groups of names whose consonants match (MOHAMMAD / MOHAMMED / MUHAMAD
style), covering 72 spellings and 224 entries; the largest has 5 spellings
across 53 uses. **346 of the 400 entries are a single word**, a first name
only, so one group can be several different drivers. These need a person who
knows the drivers; they can't be merged automatically.

## Suggested fixes (none applied)

1. **Tickets #1–#9.** If the paper gate tickets for 16–17 July exist, enter
   the drivers through *Edit container* (each change is logged with a reason).
   If not, leave them: they are history, and inventing a value is worse than a
   gap.
2. **Placeholder drivers and trucks (6 fields on tickets #38 and #58).**
   Correct them from the paper tickets through *Edit container*. The gate forms now refuse these
   values, so no new ones can be typed.
3. **Seals `0` / `NULL` / `UNKNOWN` (47 visits).** These almost certainly mean
   "no seal", which is normal for an empty box. Add an explicit *No seal*
   choice to Gate Out first, then clear the 47 values in one reviewed
   migration (statement (a) at the end of `checks.sql`). Seal validation was
   left alone on purpose: refusing these values today would stop staff from
   gating out an unsealed container.
4. **Non-standard plates.** Apart from #58 (item 2), check #38, #116, #117, #268, #344, #282, #299,
   #312 and #332 against the paper tickets; fix any typo through *Edit
   container*.
5. **Driver names.**
   - Rewrite existing names into the same form the gate forms now save
     (trimmed, single-spaced, capitals). That removes the 22 case/spacing
     groups in one reviewed migration (statement (b) in `checks.sql`).
   - Ask for the driver's full name; the form already says "full name", but
     most entries are a first name. A drivers list with autocomplete, keyed by
     national ID or phone number, would end spelling drift for good.
6. **Database guard (optional).** Once the data is clean, `CHECK` constraints
   that refuse placeholder values would stop anything bypassing the forms. Add
   them `NOT VALID` first, so existing rows don't block the migration.

## Validation added to the gate forms

Shared rules in `src/lib/validation.ts`, used by both Gate In and Gate Out:

- **Driver name** is required and may not be a placeholder (`N/A`, `unknown`,
  `-`, `0`, …). It may contain letters of any script, spaces, dots,
  apostrophes and hyphens; every name already on record passes this rule
  except the `N/A` placeholder. It is saved trimmed, single-spaced and in
  capitals, the form most existing records use, and the field shows that form
  when you leave it.
- **Truck number** is required, saved as one run of capitals and digits
  (`60-70415` becomes `6070415`), and must contain digits, not be all zeros or
  a placeholder, and have at least 4 characters. Anything other than 7 digits
  shows a hint ("Local plates have 7 digits…") but **is still accepted**, so
  foreign and older plates go through.
